# 🎬 Netflix Content Analytics — Insights & Industry Findings

## Executive Summary
Analysis of 8,807 titles across Movies and TV Shows reveals Netflix's strategic evolution from a licensed content repository into an international original-content streaming powerhouse.

---

## 1. Content Composition & Format Strategy
* **Format Split:** **69.6% Movies** (6,131 titles) vs **30.4% TV Shows** (2,676 titles).
* **Shift Towards Episodic Programming:** While movies dominate cumulative historical volume, TV show acquisitions grew from 18% of new releases in 2015 to over **36% in 2021**. Episodic series generate superior subscriber retention (lower monthly churn).
* **Movie Runtime Trends:** The median movie duration is **98 minutes** (mean: 99.57 min). Titles rarely exceed 130 minutes, aligning with optimal digital consumer attention spans.

---

## 2. Global Content Distribution & Localization
* **Top 10 Content-Producing Countries:**
  1. *United States:* 3,689 titles (41.9%)
  2. *India:* 1,046 titles (11.9%) — strong Bollywood movie presence.
  3. *United Kingdom:* 804 titles (9.1%)
  4. *Canada:* 445 titles (5.1%)
  5. *France:* 393 titles (4.5%)
  6. *Japan:* 318 titles (3.6%) — driven by Anime catalog strength.
  7. *South Korea:* 231 titles (2.6%) — fastest growing international category (K-Drama).
* **International Content Expansion:** Over **48% of the catalog** now originates outside the United States, reflecting localized content strategies for Asia-Pacific, Latin America, and EMEA markets.

---

## 3. Maturity Ratings & Audience Demographics
* **Dominant Ratings:**
  * **TV-MA (Mature Audiences):** 3,207 titles (36.4%) — Primary audience driver.
  * **TV-14 (Parents Strongly Cautioned):** 2,160 titles (24.5%)
  * **TV-PG / PG-13:** 1,438 titles (16.3%)
* **Finding:** Nearly **61% of all library content** is targeted towards adult/mature demographics (TV-MA and TV-14), providing differentiated original storytelling compared to family-focused competitors like Disney+.

---

## 4. TV Show Longevity & Renewal Dynamics
* **Single-Season Phenomenon:** Over **66.8% of TV series** in the catalog consist of exactly **1 season**.
* **Renewed Shows (2-3 Seasons):** 21.4%
* **Long-Running Franchises (4+ Seasons):** Only 11.8% of shows survive beyond 3 seasons.
* **Finding:** Netflix aggressively cancels lower-performing series after Season 1 or 2, prioritizing new intellectual property over sustaining mid-tier franchises.

---

## 5. Catalog Ingestion Seasonality
* **Peak Addition Months:** **July and December** historically record the highest volume of title additions (coinciding with summer vacations and holiday streaming spikes).
* **Lowest Months:** February exhibits the lowest release volume due to post-holiday lull.
